# Intake question bank

Ask in small batches. Record answers in chat and in `submit/case.json` / `FORM_GUIDE.md`. Unknown → `TBD`. Ask for documents as the source of truth ("send me a screenshot of the eVisa page") rather than relying on memory.

## A. Route and timing
- What are you applying for: first visa, extension, settlement (ILR), switch? Inside or outside the UK?
- Which category (partner, parent, child, Skilled Worker, student, etc.)? Same partner/employer as last time?
- Exact current permission expiry date (from the UKVI account / eVisa). Previous application reference number.
- Is the previous application available (PDF/confirmation)? It is the best source for answers that should stay consistent.
- Any target date or travel plans? (Check the official guidance on when the person may apply and what happens while it is pending; don't assume.)

## B. People
- Applicant and sponsor: full names exactly as on passports (including other/previous names), DOB, place of birth, nationality, passport number, issue/expiry.
- National Insurance numbers. Contact email and phone for each.
- Applicant's parents: names, DOBs (as on prior applications), nationalities.
- Children: names, DOB, nationality, who they live with, whether included in the application.

## C. Relationship (family routes)
- Date and place of marriage/partnership; certificate and translation available?
- Previous marriages for either partner?
- Date of arrival in the UK; living together since?

## D. Addresses
- Every address since arrival (or required period): dates, who lived there, rented/owned/family, evidence held for each person at each address.

## E. Requirements
- Financial: how is it met (salary, self-employment, savings, pension, exemption)? Employer, start date, salary history, pay frequency, payslip and bank statement availability, any salary change in the period.
- Accommodation: owned/rented, all occupants with ages, number of bedrooms and living rooms, anything ambiguous (conservatory, box room, garage conversion), landlord/agent contact.
- English: test, degree taught in English (with Ecctis/UK ENIC), nationality exemption?
- Route-specific others (e.g. sponsorship certificate, maintenance funds).

## F. History and character
- Travel outside the UK since arrival.
- Refusals, overstaying, removals, breaches of conditions anywhere.
- Criminal convictions, cautions, pending charges (any country).
- NHS debt; Home Office litigation debt.

## G. Public funds (ask explicitly)
- Has the applicant ever applied for, registered for, or received any benefit, tax credit, housing help, or council support, including Child Benefit registered in their name with payments opted out?
- Has the sponsor received benefits that might affect the financial requirement?
- If yes to anything: when, who claimed, was anything paid, is it still open? Flag for adviser review before submission.

## H. Working preferences
- Paste an example email you'd send HR / a landlord, so drafts match your voice.
- Who are the right contacts (HR inbox, letting agent, council)?
- How do you want progress tracked (just chat, a checklist file, an app)?
