# Request letter templates

Match the user's own voice. Ask them to paste an email they've sent before. Default to concise, polite, British English. Ask only for what the rules need. Set a deadline that leaves at least a week before the "submit by" date. Replace every `<...>`.

## Employer letter (salaried income)

Casual variant (typical internal HR email):

> Hey, I'm getting my documents together for my <partner's / family member's> visa application. Could you please share a signed letter on company headed paper confirming:
> - I'm employed by <Company> (employee no. <n>) and have been since <start date>
> - My job title is <title>, and it's a <permanent / fixed-term>, <full-time / part-time> role
> - My current gross annual salary is £<amount>, and the date I've been paid this from
>
> <Optional: a copy of my contract; please update my payroll address to <new address> from <date>.>
> A signed PDF is fine, ideally by <date>. Thanks!

Formal variant: same bullets, "Dear HR team" / "Kind regards".

Check the current FM-SE wording for the required fields before sending.

## Landlord / letting agent occupancy letter

> **Subject:** <Property address>: occupancy letter
>
> Dear <Agent> team,
>
> I am a tenant at <address>. I am collecting documents for a visa application. Could you please provide a signed letter on headed paper confirming:
> - The tenancy start date and monthly rent
> - The tenants: <names>
> - <Other occupants, with children's dates of birth>
> - That all <n> occupants are permitted to live at the property
> - The property's rooms: <bedrooms, living rooms, etc.>
>
> <If names are misspelt: "The tenancy agreement spells <X> as <Y>. No need to reissue it, but please use the correct spelling in the letter and update your records.">
>
> A signed PDF by email is fine, ideally by <date>.
>
> Kind regards,
> <Name> · <phone>

## Council property inspection (optional; only if room numbers are borderline)

> Dear Private Sector Housing team,
>
> I would like to request a property inspection report for immigration purposes at <address>. The household is <n> adults and <n> children (<ages>). Please let me know the fee, what you need from me, and the earliest date available. Ideally I need the report by <date>.

## HMRC: end a benefit claim made by a person with no recourse to public funds

Send by tracked post (check the current address on gov.uk). Keep a copy and the receipt. Phone first if possible and note the date and call reference for the cover letter.

> <Claimant name>, <address>, National Insurance number: <NI>
> <Date>
>
> **End of Child Benefit claim: <child name>, born <DOB>**
>
> Please end my Child Benefit claim for <child> with effect from today. I opted out of all payments and have never received any Child Benefit. <Other parent>, who is a <British citizen / settled>, will claim in their own name. Please confirm in writing that the claim has ended and that no payments were made.
>
> Yours faithfully, <signature>

Only use this after the user has had (or chosen to skip) adviser input.
